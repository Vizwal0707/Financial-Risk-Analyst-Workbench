## 1. Executive Statement of Scope

This document sets forth the explicit operational boundaries, dataset constraints, mathematical limitations, and regulatory safeguards governing the Financial Fraud Detection & Risk Intelligence Platform and its interactive Streamlit Risk Workbench.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RESEARCH PROTOTYPE DISCLAIMER**  
> This platform is an advanced quantitative research prototype engineered to demonstrate machine learning, quantitative risk modeling, and distributed data systems architecture relevant to institutional transaction monitoring (e.g., JPMorgan Chase & Co.). It is **not** a production banking application, has **not** received statutory regulatory certification, and is **strictly prohibited from connecting to live interbank payment rails, clearinghouses, or consumer credit facilities.**

## 2. Dataset Limitations & Synthetic Benchmark Scope

### 2.1 The PaySim Synthetic Benchmark
All models, feature transformations, evaluations, and simulated dashboards operate exclusively on the public **PaySim synthetic mobile money benchmark dataset** ($6,362,620$ total records):
- **Simulation Artifacts:** PaySim was generated by an agent-based simulation model (Lopez-Rojas et al., 2016) based on aggregated financial logs from an African mobile money service. The behavioral patterns, balance distributions, and fraud mechanisms reflect agent rules rather than genuine consumer banking activity.
- **Truncated 743-Hour Horizon:** The simulation represents approximately 31 days of transaction activity ($743$ hourly simulation steps). It lacks seasonal macro-economic cycles, quarterly payroll spikes, holiday shopping surges, or multi-year structural trends.
- **Concentrated Fraud Mechanisms:** Fraud occurs in PaySim exclusively across two transaction types: `TRANSFER` ($0.77\%$ fraud rate) and `CASH_OUT` ($0.18\%$ fraud rate). Routine payment types (`PAYMENT`, `CASH_IN`, `DEBIT`) contain exactly $0.00\%$ fraudulent records in the benchmark, creating an idealized partition that does not reflect real-world multi-channel payment ecosystems.

### 2.2 Absence of Geographic & Telemetry Data
- **Zero Geographic Attributes:** The dataset contains **no country codes, currency indicators, IP addresses, GPS coordinates, or regional branch identifiers**.
- **Definition of "Corridor":** In this repository, the term "corridor" strictly designates the **transaction execution mechanism / payment type** (`TRANSFER`, `CASH_OUT`, `PAYMENT`, `CASH_IN`, `DEBIT`). It must never be interpreted as an international cross-border payment corridor (e.g. US-to-Mexico wire corridor).
- **Absence of Device Telemetry:** The platform does not observe device fingerprints, browser user-agents, biometric typing cadences, or SIM swap alerts.

## 3. Machine Learning & Explainability Limitations

### 3.1 Non-Causal Nature of TreeSHAP
- **Statistical Attribution vs. Legal Causation:** Local feature attributions generated by the `ExplainerService` using TreeSHAP reflect the mathematical sensitivity of the gradient-boosted decision trees in log-odds space. A high positive SHAP value for a feature (e.g. `balance_orig_drain_ratio`) indicates that the model increased its fraud score based on statistical correlation. It does **not** constitute legal evidence of criminal culpability, fraud intent, or unauthorized account takeover.
- **Prohibition on Automated Regulatory Reporting:** The platform's "Fraud Risk Explanation Cards" are advisory summaries intended for Level-1 human investigative triage. They must never be submitted directly as automated Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to regulatory authorities (FinCEN, OCC, FCA) without independent human case investigation and corroborating evidence.

### 3.2 Imbalanced Learning & Calibration Drift
- **Calibration Specificity:** The non-parametric Isotonic Regression calibrator (`calibrator_best.joblib`) was fitted on the validation set ($N=209,984$). If the empirical baseline fraud incidence shifts significantly from the historical $\sim 0.6\% - 1.5\%$ prevalence, calibrated posterior probabilities will experience calibration drift and must be recalibrated.
- **Aspirational Benchmark Context:** Test set PR-AUC ($0.9979$) and Brier score ($0.00027$) reflect the high separability of the synthetic PaySim balance-drain fraud mechanism. In live commercial banking environments, real-world fraud is obscured by complex obfuscation techniques, and institutional PR-AUC typically ranges from $0.40$ to $0.75$.

## 4. Architectural & Operational Boundaries

### 4.1 Strict Disconnect from Live Payment Rails
- **Simulated Workflow Recommendations:** The operational recommendations emitted by the engine (`STP_APPROVE`, `QUEUE_L1_REVIEW`, `BLOCK_TRANSACTION`, `REQUIRE_STEP_UP_AUTHENTICATION`) are simulated workflow tags. They are accompanied by `simulated_recommendation_only: True` and explicit disclaimers.
- **Prohibited Infrastructure Connections:** Under no circumstances should this service be interfaced with live clearinghouse rails, including:
  - Federal Reserve Fedwire Funds Service
  - Clearing House Interbank Payments System (CHIPS)
  - Automated Clearing House (ACH / NACHA)
  - SWIFT FIN / ISO 20022 messaging networks
  - Card payment network authorization networks (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  - Real-Time Payments (RTP) or FedNow rails

### 4.2 Single-Node Workstation Reference Environment
- **Benchmark Context:** The throughput figures ($126.6\text{ req/s}$ single-stream, $254.2\text{ tx/s}$ batch) and latency measurements ($1.05\text{ ms}$ core inference) were captured on a single reference workstation (Darwin x86_64).
- **Production Scaling Differences:** Live deployment would require multi-worker Uvicorn clusters fronted by an API Gateway, distributed cache nodes (Redis), and an enterprise PostgreSQL or CockroachDB cluster.

### 4.3 Policy Simulator Boundaries
- **Offline Evaluation Scope:** The interactive policy simulator operates exclusively on the pre-scored offline evaluation dataset (`test_evaluation_scored.parquet`).
- **Zero Production Mutation:** Adjusting sliders in the Streamlit UI does **not** alter the frozen production model weights, feature transformations, or active FastAPI thresholds ($\tau_{\text{low}}=0.10, \tau_{\text{high}}=0.75$). All hypothetical cost analyses are labeled as non-binding scenario tests.

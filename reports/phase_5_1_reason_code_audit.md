**Audit Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Audit Scope:** Technical Verification of Reason-Code Derivation, Evidence Traceability, and Decision Policy Governance  
**Auditor Role:** Independent Model Validation & Financial Risk Architect  
**Evaluation Target:** Real-Time Fraud Decisioning Engine (`src/decision_engine/policy_engine.py`)  
**Status:** **AUDITED AND VERIFIED**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

In financial crime risk management (AML/BSA and transaction monitoring), automated model scores cannot operate as opaque black boxes. Regulatory frameworks (such as OCC Bulletin 2011-12 / Federal Reserve SR 11-7 on Model Risk Management) require that high-risk automated interventions and referrals to investigative staff be accompanied by clear, transparent, and legally defensible explanatory reason codes.

This Phase 5.1 audit examined the reason-code generation mechanism within the transaction scoring engine. Specifically, this audit evaluated:
1. **Evidence Grounding:** Every reason code is traced to specific, validated input features available strictly at decision time.
2. **Rule vs. Model Separation:** Distinguishing heuristic policy triggers from machine learning anomaly detections.
3. **Suppression Guardrails:** Correct suppression of invalid reason codes during cold-start scenarios and payee classification.
4. **Non-Geographic Corridor Definition:** Explicit clarification that the PaySim benchmark contains zero geographic data; the term "corridor" denotes transaction mechanism types (`TRANSFER`, `CASH_OUT`), not geographic jurisdictions.

## 3. Disallowed and Removed Reason Codes

During the Phase 5.1 audit, the codebase was inspected for legacy, unsupported, or misleading reason codes:

1. **`GEOGRAPHIC_CORRIDOR_ANOMALY` — REMOVED / DISALLOWED:**
   - *Audit Finding:* Earlier prototype drafts suggested corridor-based risk tags.
   - *Finding Detail:* The PaySim synthetic benchmark has **zero geographic, country-code, IP address, or regional jurisdiction attributes**.
   - *Verification Action:* All references to geographic corridors were strictly purged. All corridor reason codes (`CORRIDOR_TRANSFER_HIGH_RISK`, `CORRIDOR_CASH_OUT_HIGH_RISK`) are explicitly documented as referring to **payment transaction types / execution mechanisms**, not geopolitical regions.

2. **`DEVICE_FINGERPRINT_MISMATCH` — REMOVED / DISALLOWED:**
   - *Audit Finding:* No device telemetry (cookie, user-agent, Canvas fingerprint, IMEI) exists in the PaySim dataset.
   - *Verification Action:* Confirmed that no device-derived reason codes exist in the policy engine.

3. **`BENEFICIARY_WATCHLIST_MATCH` — REMOVED / DISALLOWED:**
   - *Audit Finding:* Sanctions screening (OFAC/SDN) is an AML screening process distinct from transaction fraud risk. No external watchlists exist in PaySim.
   - *Verification Action:* Confirmed absent from the scoring service.

## 4. Evidentiary Guardrail Validations

The audit verified two critical algorithmic guardrails in the reason code engine:

### 4.1 Merchant Mule Suppression
In electronic payment ecosystems, commercial merchant accounts (`M...` entities in PaySim) naturally receive inbound consumer payments. Because PaySim does not simulate merchant bank balance ledgers, their recorded balances frequently read $0.00$. 

- **Audit Test:** Scored an authentic merchant payment (`nameDest = M9000000001`, `oldbalanceDest = 0.0`).
- **Observed Behavior:** `DEST_ZERO_BALANCE_MULE` was successfully suppressed. The transaction was classified with `LOW_RISK_MERCHANT_PAYMENT` and approved via `STP_APPROVE`.
- **Pass Status:** **VERIFIED** (Automated regression test: `test_merchant_payment_never_triggers_zero_balance_mule`).

### 4.2 Cold-Start Velocity Suppression
When a transaction is scored without historical context (`allow_cold_start = True`), the engine initializes historical counters to neutral baseline defaults.

- **Audit Test:** Scored cold-start transactions with default counters.
- **Observed Behavior:** The policy engine explicitly checked `if not cold_start_applied:` before evaluating velocity thresholds. Neither `ELEVATED_VELOCITY_1H` nor `ELEVATED_VELOCITY_24H` was emitted.
- **Pass Status:** **VERIFIED** (Automated regression test: `test_cold_start_suppresses_velocity_reason_codes`).

## 5. Audit Conclusion

The reason-code framework implemented in `src/decision_engine/policy_engine.py` meets institutional risk standards:
- All 11 reason codes are 100% grounded in validated transaction or model inputs.
- No fabricated or synthetic indicators are produced.
- Geographic terminology is eliminated, and payment mechanisms are explicitly identified.
- Automated tests enforce guardrails against false mule and velocity alerts.
